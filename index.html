Quick Transformation

From trailer to hand truck in seconds

The Packer can be transformed from a bike trailer to a hand truck in just a few seconds. The innovative folding mechanism enables quick switching between both modes without tools. This way you can comfortably cycle to the shops, convert the trailer into a hand truck on site and transport all your purchases without problems - even over stairs and through narrow doors.

Max. Load50-200 kg
Assembly Time~30 seconds

Maximum Flexibility

Convenient transport of Euro boxes

The Packer is specially optimized for transporting standardized 600x400 mm Euro boxes. This compatibility opens up unlimited possibilities for transporting goods, tools or personal items. The boxes can be securely fastened and provide additional weather protection. With the Packer you can transport up to 50 kg in trailer mode and up to 120 kg in hand truck mode.

Max. Load120 kg
Assembly Time~30 seconds

Practical in Everyday Life

Transport groceries and more easily

The Packer is your perfect companion for everyday transport tasks. From bulk shopping to beverage crates to bulky items - with the Packer you transport everything effortlessly and environmentally friendly. The universal coupling fits any bike and can be attached without tools. After use, you can disassemble the Packer to save space and store it in a Euro box.

Max. Load50-200 kg
Assembly Time~30 seconds

Urban Mobility

Sustainable travel in the city

The Packer revolutionizes urban mobility and makes cars unnecessary in the city. With it you can travel environmentally friendly and traffic-jam-free while still fulfilling all your transport needs. The smart Packer app offers additional functions such as theft protection, side distance sensor and maintenance reminders. Thanks to local and sustainable production, the Packer contributes to reducing CO2 emissions.

Max. Load50-200 kg
Assembly Time~30 seconds
